How it's measured

Beacon is a capability-normalized index. Rather than averaging sticker prices — which only fall and hide what you actually get — it tracks the lowest $/million-tokens to reach a fixed capability tier (scored on GPQA-Diamond) across every major model. The construction is rules-based, reproducible, and transparent, the way credible commodity and rate benchmarks are built.

Secured on-chain

A dashboard tells a human what AI costs. Beacon publishes the rate on-chain, where any contract or agent can settle against it — and secures it economically: publishers stake BEACON on the accuracy of their feeds, a multi-publisher median sets the rate, and submissions that deviate from it are automatically slashed. That last part — money at risk for bad data — is what a free price tracker can't offer.
